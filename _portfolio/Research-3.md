excerpt: "In today's contested radio-frequency environment, high-fidelity wireless communication is both more important 
than ever, and more difficult to maintain. Furthermore, bad actors can jam crucial signals sent to self-driving cars, autonomous UAV
and manned aircraft. AutoWav, an AFRL-sponsored research project, seeks to mitigate RF interference by avoiding it entirely by using neural networks to redesign waveforms 
in real time. AutoWav has resulted in a number of waveform design techniques, culminating in >1000X speedup over conventional convex optimization for
waveform design.
